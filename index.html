Promo Video Maker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Promo Video Maker ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that turns the product information on a web page into a short promo video. This policy explains what the extension does and doesn't do with your information.

What the extension reads

When you click the extension's toolbar icon, it reads the page you're viewing to find the business name, product name, price, a short description and product photos. It only does this on the page where you clicked, and only at that moment.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Videos are created entirely inside your browser. Page content, photos, the text you type and the videos you make are never sent to us or to anyone else.

If you allow the extension to load photos from a website, it downloads those photos directly from that website into your browser, the same way the page does.

What is stored on your device

  • The page details from your last click, kept briefly so the editor tab can show them and then deleted.
  • If you're a Pro user, the custom watermark text you entered.

This information stays in your browser's extension storage and is removed if you uninstall the extension.

Payments

Pro upgrades are processed by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe. If you upgrade, they collect your email address and payment details to process the payment and keep track of your subscription. We never see your card details. See ExtensionPay's and Stripe's privacy policies for how they handle that data.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use your data for advertising or creditworthiness.
